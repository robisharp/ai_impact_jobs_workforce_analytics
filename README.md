## 📊 Project Overview

The **AI Impact on Jobs & Workforce Analytics** project is an interactive data visualization dashboard designed to analyze how Artificial Intelligence is influencing the workforce across different industries.

The dashboard provides insights into **AI adoption, employee demographics, salary changes, productivity impact, automation risk, and upskilling requirements**.

The project focuses on transforming workforce-related data into meaningful visual insights that can support better understanding of the changing employment landscape.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

- Analyze the level of AI adoption across employees and industries.
- Compare average salaries before and after AI adoption.
- Understand changes in employee productivity.
- Analyze workforce demographics.
- Identify industries with different levels of automation risk.
- Analyze employee upskilling requirements.
- Compare AI adoption levels with job status.
- Provide an interactive dashboard for workforce analysis.

---

## 📌 Key Dashboard Insights

The dashboard includes the following key metrics and visualizations:

### 1. Employees Count
Displays the total number of employees included in the dataset.

### 2. Average Salary After AI
Shows the average salary of employees after AI adoption.

### 3. Average Productivity Change %
Measures the average percentage change in employee productivity after AI adoption.

### 4. Average Salary Before AI
Displays the average salary before AI adoption.

### 5. Demographic Analysis
Analyzes employee distribution based on age and employee count.

### 6. Salary Before vs After AI
Compares employee salaries before and after AI across different industries such as:

- Healthcare
- Marketing
- Retail
- Education
- Finance
- Manufacturing
- IT

### 7. AI Adoption vs Job Status
Analyzes employee distribution according to different AI adoption levels and job status.

### 8. AI Adoption Level & Productivity
Visualizes the relationship between AI adoption and productivity change.

### 9. Upskilling Requirement
Shows the proportion of employees who require additional upskilling due to changes brought by AI.

### 10. Automation Risk by Industry
Compares automation risk across different industries and helps identify areas where AI-driven automation may have a greater workforce impact.
